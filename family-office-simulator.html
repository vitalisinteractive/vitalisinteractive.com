Family Office Simulator

Build and run a family office across real estate, capital, staffing, governance, and succession.

Play on Steam Steam Community
The game

Build more than a balance sheet.

Acquire property, manage debt and liquidity, hire staff, establish trusts and policies, and prepare the next generation. The systems interact, and decisions made early can follow the office for decades.

Major systems

One institution. Many moving parts.

Real estate

Acquire, finance, operate, and sell income-producing property.

Capital structure

Balance leverage, liquidity, banking, brokerage, and risk.

People

Hire the team, manage compensation, and build operating capacity.

Governance

Use trusts, policies, succession, and philanthropy to shape the institution.

Time

Live with market cycles and the consequences of earlier choices.

Legacy

Prepare heirs and test whether the institution survives its founder.
