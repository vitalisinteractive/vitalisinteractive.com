Property management

Track what each property is actually doing.

Owned property is not a single return percentage. Occupancy, signed rents, operating expenses, debt, insurance, unit turns, and market conditions affect what the asset produces and what you can do next.

Examples from the current Early Access build

Preview financing before an offer

Enter a hypothetical purchase price and compare all-cash and eligible lender options before the first seller-facing offer. The preview shows cash to close, remaining checking, debt service, DSCR, fees, recourse, lockouts, and exact eligibility blockers.

Repeated rent increases add up

Occupied-rent proposals show the market comparison and move-out exposure before confirmation. Recent increases are evaluated across a rolling 12-month period, so several small increases cannot sidestep the risk created by the total increase.

Staff capacity changes what gets handled

An in-house Property Manager can process eligible routine residential renewals when delegation, policy, onboarding, and team capacity all allow it. If the work falls outside those limits, it comes back to you.
