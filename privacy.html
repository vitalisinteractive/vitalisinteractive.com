Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vitalis Interactive handles information from this website, correspondence, and records from our former email newsletter.

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Who we are

Vitalis Interactive LLC is an independent game studio based in the United States. Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to hello@vitalisinteractive.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers vitalisinteractive.com, correspondence with Vitalis Interactive, and records retained from our former email newsletter. The HALIVRO early-access list has a separate website privacy policy. External services you visit through links have their own privacy practices.

Information you choose to provide

We receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include when you email us about support, business, creator outreach, or other inquiries. The retired newsletter may have historical subscription and consent records held by our email service provider. This website no longer accepts newsletter subscriptions.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages, which may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP addresses, browser information, and request logs. We no longer embed a newsletter signup form or reCAPTCHA on this website.

Website analytics and Metricool

We use Metricool to record aggregate website visits and page views. According to Metricool’s tracker documentation, the tracker does not use cookies, store visitor IP addresses, or assign unique identifiers. It uses a visitor’s IP address transiently to determine the visitor’s country and then discards the IP address.

How we use information

  • To honor historical email preferences, unsubscribe requests, and applicable recordkeeping obligations.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect Vitalis Interactive, its players, and its services.

We do not sell your personal information.

Retired newsletter records

Our former game-newsletter signup used Brevo for confirmation and email delivery. New subscriptions are no longer accepted on this website. Brevo may retain limited consent, delivery, bounce, or unsubscribe records from the former service to honor requests and legal obligations. This does not enroll visitors in HALIVRO emails.

When information is shared

We share information only as reasonably necessary with service providers that help operate the website, analytics, email system, and business; when required by law; or when needed to protect rights, safety, and security. Our providers include Metricool for website analytics, GitHub Pages for hosting, and Microsoft 365 for email. Brevo may still process historical newsletter and suppression records.

How long information is kept

Historical newsletter consent and suppression records are retained only as needed to honor requests, maintain necessary records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Correspondence is kept only as long as it remains useful for support, business, legal, accounting, or security purposes.

Your choices

If you previously received newsletter emails, you can use the unsubscribe link in those messages. You may also contact hello@vitalisinteractive.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your email address. Some records may be retained when required by law or needed to document an unsubscribe request.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly solicit their personal information.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate for a small independent studio. No method of online transmission or storage is completely secure, but access to business systems is limited and account protections are used where available.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the studio, website, or service providers change. The date at the top of this page will show the latest revision.
