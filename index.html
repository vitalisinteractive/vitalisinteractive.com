Family Office Simulator

What you actually manage.

A property purchase affects more than net worth. Financing changes liquidity and debt service. Rent decisions can raise income or increase move-out risk. Hiring replaces some outside costs, but adds payroll and only helps when the team has capacity to do the work.

See gameplay and decision examples →

Research a deal without losing the negotiation

Review financing, switch apps, save, and return without losing the seller’s counter, your last offer, the round count, or accepted terms. The agreed purchase price stays locked through financing and closing.

Rent changes can cost you the tenant

Occupied-rent proposals show market comparison and move-out exposure. Repeated increases inside a rolling 12-month window count together instead of resetting the risk each time.

Staff have authority and workload

An in-house Property Manager can handle eligible routine renewals when policy, onboarding, and team capacity allow it. Otherwise the decision comes back to you.

Why Vitalis builds this way.

I started Vitalis because I wanted business games where growth creates new responsibilities instead of just bigger numbers. Real-world detail stays when it creates an interesting decision, explains an outcome, or teaches you something useful about the business.
