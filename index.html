The Vitalis Standard

Games built around the system—not around the spectacle.

Every Vitalis project can look different. The standards underneath stay the same.

A one-person studio, by design.

There are fewer layers between a player report and the next build. That makes honest feedback useful—and careful priorities necessary.

Recognizable systems

Mechanics draw from how businesses, machines, organizations, and ownership actually function.
