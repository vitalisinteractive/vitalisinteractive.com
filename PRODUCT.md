# Vitalis Interactive Website — Product Truth

## Purpose
Present Vitalis Interactive as a serious independent simulation studio and help visitors understand, evaluate, follow, and buy/wishlist its games.

## Audience
- Players who value management, economy, business, and systems-driven simulation.
- Existing Family Office Simulator players looking for current product information and updates.
- Prospective players evaluating Main Street Operator and Dealer Principal.
- Press, creators, business contacts, and support visitors.

## Primary jobs
1. Make the games understandable quickly.
2. Show real game evidence rather than abstract brand claims.
3. Send buyers/wishlisters to the correct Steam pages.
4. Keep public product state accurate.
5. Provide a clean route to current updates, support, and contact information.

## Content authority
- Steam is the canonical source for complete Family Office Simulator patch notes.
- The website curates meaningful recent updates and links to Steam for the full feed.
- Public claims must match shipped/publicly announced behavior.
- Internal integrations, unreleased candidates, and roadmap experiments are not public facts.

## Design character
Premium, deliberate, operational, simulation-first, and grounded. The site should look like a studio that understands the businesses it simulates, not like a generic SaaS or AI startup template.

## Non-goals
- Do not turn the homepage into a changelog.
- Do not expose internal development mechanics, agent tooling, private metrics, or unreleased features.
- Do not chase visual novelty at the expense of clarity, credibility, or game evidence.
