# Vitalis Interactive Website Design System

## Positioning
Vitalis creates grounded simulation games inspired by how the real world actually works.

## Visual direction
An established institution crossed with an honest indie-development workbench: serious, editorial, game-first, and alive.

## Official identity
- The approved 2026 identity is the exact black-and-gold artwork supplied by the studio owner.
- Do not redraw, reinterpret, simplify, restyle, or regenerate the V or the VITALIS INTERACTIVE lockup.
- Use the V-only mark in the header, favicon, and small icon contexts.
- Use the full horizontal lockup in the footer and selected studio-brand moments.
- The Studio page may feature the exact V and horizontal lockup at larger scale.
- Gold is an accent and material cue, not a page-filling theme. The games remain the primary visual evidence.
- Keep archived identity files for history, but do not surface older marks on the public site.

## Rules
- Use real gameplay and dated studio activity before decorative mockups.
- Dark teal/ink, warm paper, and restrained gold are the core palette.
- Public copy must be plainspoken, specific, and supported by the game or studio activity.
- Avoid formulaic AI-writing patterns, abstract brand manifestos, repeated three-part slogans, and generic corporate filler.
- Editorial serif headlines may carry emotion; body copy must use ordinary verbs and natural sentence rhythm.
- Avoid repeated eyebrow/heading/card formulas across every section.
- Do not repeatedly use eyebrow + giant heading + paragraph + equal cards as a page template.
- Avoid glassmorphism, excessive glow, fake app windows, floating badges, and generic AI gradients.
- Use asymmetry and varied section structures.
- Keep buttons square-edged or subtly rounded; no pill-heavy UI.
- Every public claim must be supported by visible game, update, review, or community evidence.
- Use real gameplay evidence ahead of abstract claims.
- Avoid volatile proof points such as live review counts unless they are automated or deliberately maintained.
- Do not publicly name or promote an unrevealed game without explicit owner approval.
- Do not expose a founder's personal name or address by default.
- No public Portfolio Playthrough promotion while the initiative is paused.
- Legal and support pages prioritize clarity over clever branding.
- Direct sales may be designed for, but no purchase CTA may appear until checkout, fulfillment, refund, privacy, and support systems are ready.

## Typography
- Headlines: Georgia / Times fallback.
- Interface/body: Arial / Helvetica fallback.
- Use large type sparingly; one dominant statement per section.

## Contact
- General: hello@vitalisinteractive.com
- Support: support@vitalisinteractive.com
- Founder address is not the default public contact.

## Responsive
Mobile layouts must preserve hierarchy, real imagery, readable tap targets, and logo legibility without decorative overflow.
